The challenge

Competing demands, limited hours

Many demands at once

Operations teams juggle inspections, customer requests, contractor follow-ups and admin, often across thousands of units each.

Loudest is not most urgent

The most frequent complaint is not automatically the priority. Genuine safety risks and mandatory deadlines are.

Plans that do not fit the day

Urgent work arrives mid-day. Without a realistic plan, it is hard to see what can move and what cannot.

Operationally aware AI scheduling

Understand priorities. Build achievable plans. Explain the changes.

Priority logic

Ranked by what matters on the ground

Four operational factors, weighed together and tunable to your organisation’s own rules.

  1. 1

    Safety

    Genuine risks to people, staff and property.

  2. 2

    Mandatory deadlines

    Statutory, regulatory, contractual and funding-approval dates that cannot slip.

  3. 3

    Service impact & long-term consequences

    Real customer needs and what happens if the work waits. Not who complains loudest.

  4. 4

    Scheduling feasibility

    Estimated task durations matched to each person’s free time and capacity.

How it works

From a long task list to an approved plan

  1. Step 1

    Sign in with Microsoft

    Use your organisation’s Microsoft account. No separate SyncAI password.

  2. Step 2

    Review your plan

    SyncAI ranks your tasks and drafts a day that fits around your Outlook meetings.

  3. Step 3

    Approve the changes

    Move anything you like, then accept. Nothing changes until you or your manager say so.
