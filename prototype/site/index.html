Proposed product differentiators

Three pillars of the PS 12i Smart Work Planner

Built to address the daily realities of EM Services estate management with transparent, explainable assistance.

1. Operationally Aware AI Scheduling

Considers real estate management priorities: genuine resident safety hazards, statutory deadlines (BCA/SCDF), and service impact. Repeat complaint volume receives 0 extra urgency points.

2. Explainable What-If Replanning

Simulate unexpected disruptions—such as an urgent lift breakdown or officer medical leave—before committing changes. See affected tasks, preserved statutory obligations, and clear reasons for every move.

3. Capacity-Aware Planning

Assesses whether tasks realistically fit into true available working hours around Outlook meetings, inspections, and mandatory travel buffer. Flags impossible constraints instead of silently overpromising.

The problem

Estate work never arrives in a neat queue

Town Council officers juggle safety inspections, resident feedback, contractor follow-ups and back-to-back meetings. Plans live in people's heads, so problems surface only when a deadline is already missed.

"What should I do first?"

Priorities shift through the day and urgent cases keep arriving. Officers lose time deciding instead of doing.

"Can I finish all this today?"

Calendars show meetings, not work. Nobody can see whether the remaining tasks actually fit in the free time left.

"Who can take over?"

When an officer is on MC or a lift breaks down, supervisors rebalance work by phone and spreadsheet.

Operationally aware AI scheduling

Ranked the way EM Services Property Officers really work

A loud complaint is not the same as an urgent one. SyncAI weighs genuine safety risks, mandatory statutory deadlines, service impact and long-term consequences against realistic working capacity. All numerical rules are clearly labeled as configurable prototype assumptions.

Genuine safety hazards (+20)

Explicitly identifies falling concrete, lift entrapments, exposed wiring, and fire hazards. Safety always precedes general maintenance.

Mandatory deadlines (+25 / +15)

BCA annual lift certifications and statutory compliance deadlines are treated as hard scheduling constraints, not just numerical bonuses.

Long-term consequences (+12)

Flags severe escalation risks such as ceiling water seepage penetrating structural rebar, void deck pooling, or regulatory stop-work orders.

Repeat complaints: 0 pts

Repeated resident calls add zero priority points. SyncAI protects officers from loudest-voice bias while maintaining professional response standards.

Feasibility & impossible conflicts

Calculates required task effort against Outlook meetings, lunch, and mandatory travel buffer. Proactively warns when a deadline is mathematically unachievable.

Configurable assumptions

Every score factor is clearly labeled as an illustrative prototype model assumption, ready for calibration against EM Services operational benchmarks.

FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

Does SyncAI replace our existing tools?

No. It sits on top of Microsoft 365. Tasks, meetings and alerts stay in Outlook, Planner and Teams; SyncAI adds the planning and the AI.

Can the AI move my work without asking?

Never. SyncAI drafts plans and suggestions. Only the officer or supervisor can accept them, and every approval is logged.

Who can see my tasks?

You and your supervisor. Officers never see other officers' workload, and absences show only as MC or leave.

How long does setup take?

A pilot team can start in a day: sign in with Microsoft, connect the calendar, and SyncAI builds the first plan from existing tasks.
